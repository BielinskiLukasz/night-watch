## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)

- **ANTI-PATTERN: innerHTML with dynamic data** → All dynamic DOM in js/ must use `textContent` or `el({textContent: ...})`. The XSS guard in dom.js is a load-bearing invariant.
- **ANTI-PATTERN: calling `new Date()` in modules** → Always use `clock.now()` from the injected clock adapter. Modules in lib/ and store/ never call new Date() directly.
