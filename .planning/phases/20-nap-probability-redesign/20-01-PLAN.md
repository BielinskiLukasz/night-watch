<objective>
Rewrite `napProbability()`'s signal engine in `js/lib/forecast.js`: remove the two clock-based
signals (`elapsedWakeTime`, `windowPassed`), add two data-driven signals (`dayOfWeekNapRate`,
`sleepDebtSignal`) sourced from `js/lib/metrics.js`, retain `napFrequency`/`noNapStreak`'s
computation under new weights, and change the return contract from a bare `null|0|number` to
`{ score, signalsUsed, confidence }` (D-01..D-10, NAP-01..04).

Purpose: the shipped v1.3 score drifts as the day progresses (clock-based inputs); this phase
makes it stable once computed at wake time and grounds it in the user's actual weekday/sleep-debt
patterns instead of elapsed clock time.

Output: `NAP_SCORE_WEIGHTS` (4 keys, sums to 1.0), rewritten `napProbability()`, extended
`dayOfWeekAverages()`, and a full test suite proving the engine end-to-end.
</objective>
