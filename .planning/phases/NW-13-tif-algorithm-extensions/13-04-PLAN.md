<objective>
TDD plan — add no-nap-day substitution logic to tifForecast. When the caller signals that today is a no-nap day (isNoNapDay=true), TIF substitutes historically-filtered arrays for the bedtime Day-length band and wake sleep-length band. It also skips the combined-band correction for wake (irrelevant without a nap). When yesterday was a no-nap day, TIF adds a post-no-nap nap-start pattern window derived from days that followed historical no-nap days.

Purpose: TIF-16 makes TIF predictions situationally aware on atypical days, using evidence from similar historical days rather than the entire mixed population.

Output: Three new conditional behaviours inside tifForecast, all guarded by isNoNapDay and minDays thresholds; thin sub-window fallback matches existing degradation pattern.
</objective>
