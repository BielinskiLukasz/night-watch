# Quick Task 260829-w16: Move Algorithm Selector to Top of Forecast Fieldset Summary

**One-liner:** Moved forecastAlgorithm select to top of Forecast & Prediction fieldset; wrapped classic-only fields in #classicOptions div with symmetric show/hide logic.

## What Was Built

Restructured the Settings modal's Forecast & Prediction fieldset so:

1. The forecastAlgorithm select appears first (was buried at the bottom inside a tif-subsection wrapper)
2. Classic-only fields (autoOutlier, maxDelta, windowDays, statBlend) are wrapped in `<div id="classicOptions">` and hidden when TIF is selected
3. Shared fields (minDays, eveningHour, noNapBedtimeOffsetMinutes) remain always visible
4. TIF-only fields remain in `<div id="tifOptions" hidden>` as before

The tif-subsection wrapper div and tif-subsection-label paragraph were removed entirely.

Updated `js/ui/settings-modal.js` in two places:
- `populateForm()` now sets `classicOptionsEl.hidden` in addition to `tifOptionsEl.hidden` on modal open
- `_forecastAlgorithmChangeHandler` now toggles both `#classicOptions` and `#tifOptions` on algorithm switch
