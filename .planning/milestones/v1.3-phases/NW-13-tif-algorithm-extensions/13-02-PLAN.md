<objective>
TDD plan — extend the three band-builder functions and buildPrediction to carry per-window medians, and change buildPrediction's central time to be the average of window medians.

Purpose: TIF-15 makes predictions more informative by anchoring the displayed central time to the statistical median of historical data, not the midpoint of the display range. All subsequent plans (Plan 03 ratio windows, Plan 04 no-nap bands) will produce windows with medians automatically because they use buildHistoricBand and buildDurationBand.

Output: trimmedMinMax, buildHistoricBand, buildDurationBand each return median; buildPrediction derives central from avg(medians); sourceWindows entries expose median: 'HH:MM'.
</objective>
